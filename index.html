Hodges Ward Elliott (“HWE”) is pleased to present the opportunity to acquire the Home2 Suites Naples I-75 Pine Ridge Road (the “Hotel” or “Property”), a 99-suite, extended-stay hotel located in one of the nation’s most affluent and desirable coastal markets. Recently recognized by U.S. News as the #1 Best Place to Live in America (2024 - 2025), Naples continues to attract affluent residents, businesses, and visitors drawn to its exceptional quality of life and year-round coastal appeal. The market’s continued evolution is supported by significant investments in healthcare, corporate infrastructure, and ultra-luxury residential development, further diversifying demand beyond its established tourism base.

The Property’s contemporary, all-suite accommodations, efficient operating model, and strategic location provide a compelling opportunity to acquire a top asset in one of the country’s most sought-after real estate markets.

Investment Highlights

Recent Vintage Extended-Stay Product with Superior Structural Quality

Built in 2020, the Property represents Naples’ only Hilton-branded extended-stay hotel, offering a modern, all-suite product with in-room kitchens and contemporary amenities. Notably, the Hotel features reinforced concrete masonry unit (CMU) construction, distinguishing it from the wood-frame construction commonly utilized for Home2 Suites properties. The combination of institutional construction quality, an all-suite format, and the recent vintage provides investors with a high-quality physical asset in the near term, while supporting sustained asset preservation and additional exit optionality in the long term. Coupled with the operating efficiencies inherent to the extended-stay model and the strength of Hilton’s global distribution platform, the Property offers an attractive mix of operational flexibility, cash flow potential, and long-term investment appeal.

Premier Hospitality Market with Exceptional Pricing Power

Following a period of post-pandemic normalization, Naples continues to demonstrate exceptional lodging fundamentals, supported by its position as one of the nation’s premier luxury destinations. Naples’ trailing 12-month ADR reached $359, representing a 53.0% increase over 2019 levels, while trailing 12-month RevPAR reached $233, the highest of any mainland Florida market. This sustained pricing strength underscores Naples’ ability to command premium rates and outperform competing destinations.

Approximately 67% of Collier County’s land area is designated for conservation, limiting the land available for new hotel development. Ongoing mixed-use, healthcare, and luxury development is further enhancing the market’s appeal and diversifying its lodging demand base. Year-to-date through October 2026, ADR has increased 12.2%, while RevPAR has grown 14.5%, demonstrating continued momentum in an already high-performing lodging market.

Nationally Acclaimed Luxury Market

Naples represents one of the most affluent and nationally recognized luxury markets in the United States, earning U.S. News & World Report’s #1 rankings for both Best Place to Live and Best Place to Retire. The City of Naples’ median household income of $153,182 is more than double the statewide median, retiree households average $93,499 in annual retirement income, and Naples-Marco Island now ranks as the third-most expensive luxury housing market in the country. This concentration of wealth supports a world-class retail landscape anchored by Waterside Shops, where Saks Fifth Avenue is joined by Hermès, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, Dior, and Van Cleef & Arpels, complemented by the boutiques, galleries, and fine dining of Fifth Avenue South and Third Street South.

Capital continues to flow into the market, highlighted by The Ritz-Carlton Residences, Naples, which is introducing 128 luxury residences near Vanderbilt Beach, and The Avenue, which secured $202 million in construction financing for 50 luxury residences above 75,000 square feet of retail and dining on Fifth Avenue South. This sustained investment supports durable, high-rated lodging demand across the market.

Strategic Position Near Alligator Alley and Naples’ Primary Employers

Located immediately off I-75, the Hotel is strategically positioned near the western gateway to Alligator Alley. This ~80-mile stretch of I-75 cuts straight across the Florida Everglades, connecting Naples on the west coast to Fort Lauderdale on the east coast. Commercial exits are extremely limited along Alligator Alley and development of any kind is heavily constrained by the protected Everglades ecosystem. As a result, travelers have virtually no lodging options when crossing the corridor and must stay in established markets at either end.

Beyond its transient drive-to appeal and renowned white-sand beaches, Naples also benefits from major corporate and healthcare employers such as Arthrex and Naples Comprehensive Health, youth-sports tournament activity at Paradise Coast Sports Complex, and private aviation through Naples Airport, supporting weekday, extended-stay, and leisure demand.
